Creative Direction

Designing With Purpose.

I am Sharat Gandhi, an independent freelance graphic designer dedicated to crafting clear, distinctive, and purposeful visual assets. Rather than chasing fleeting internet trends or loading layouts with arbitrary decoration, my work centers on visual communication that solves real business challenges.

My practice is grounded in a deep passion for typography, balanced composition, and cohesive brand systems. I believe every visual detail should serve a purpose—whether that means guiding an audience's eye across an editorial social slide, commanding trust through an understated mark, or producing high-converting campaign collateral.

When we collaborate, I invest time to thoroughly dissect your project requirements and understand your audience before producing visual concepts. The result is work that feels authentic, professional, and built for lasting impact.

Methodology

My Creative Process.

A disciplined four-phase framework that eliminates guesswork and delivers purposeful creative work on schedule.

01

Understanding

Understanding the client's business, audience, positioning, and functional design requirements before producing visual ideas.

02

Research

Exploring visual directions, typographic historical references, moodboards, and composition hierarchies to establish creative clarity.

03

Designing

Turning the chosen direction into thoughtful visual solutions. Iterating on typographic balance, contrast, and layout systems.

04

Delivery

Refining every detail and delivering organized, production-ready vector, digital, and print files ready for immediate deployment.
